Datacenter Empire — Support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback? Email cadpack.support@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How to play: start with Grandpa's old computer in Mom's apartment above Lucky Wok, then walk your founder around to build racks, power and cooling, move to bigger buildings, and grow all the way to space. Tap anywhere to earn while you play; your data centers keep earning while you're away.

Music, sounds and vibration: You menu → Settings.

Ad privacy choices: You menu → Settings → Ad privacy options (where available), or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. More on the ad privacy choices page.

Parody: Datacenter Empire is a small, affectionate satire made by a tech enthusiast looking ahead to the future these companies are building. It’s inspired by real achievements in AI, space and computing: the projects and people behind them are some of my favourites, and I’m excited to see the finished versions of what they’re working on. Some of the humour is inspired by crypto culture and its jokes. All company names, product names and characters are fictional parodies, not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any real company or person. In-game stock prices are made up and nothing here is financial advice. The humour is meant to be lighthearted; no offence is intended. If anyone takes offence at any of the jokes, please contact me right away at cadpack.support@gmail.com and I’ll apologize and remove it from the game.
