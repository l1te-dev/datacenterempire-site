Datacenter Empire — Support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback? Email cadpack.support@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How to play: start with Grandpa's old computer in Mom's apartment above Lucky Wok, then walk your founder around to build racks, power and cooling, move to bigger buildings, and grow all the way to space. Tap anywhere to earn while you play; your data centers keep earning while you're away.

Music, sounds and vibration: You menu → Settings.

Ad privacy choices: You menu → Settings → Ad privacy options (where available), or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. More on the ad privacy choices page.
