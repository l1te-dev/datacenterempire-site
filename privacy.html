Datacenter Empire — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Datacenter Empire is a single-player idle game. We do not run accounts or servers, and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

Game data. Your progress, purchases, settings and saved game are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.

Ads. The game offers optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID, if you allow tracking), IP address (for approximate location), ad interaction and diagnostic data, to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. If you decline tracking in the iOS prompt, you still can watch ads, but they are not personalized by cross-app tracking. In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for ads in the game (You menu → Settings → Ad privacy options). See Google's ad policy and our ad privacy choices page.

Purchases. Optional in-app purchases (such as the COO) are processed by Apple. We receive no payment information.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices. You can turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Deleting the app deletes your saved game.
